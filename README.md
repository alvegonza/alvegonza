# Álvaro Vega González

I'm doing the MSc in Logic, Computation and AI at Universidad de Sevilla. Before
that, Computer Engineering at Universidad de Jaén.

What I care about in every project is finding out where things break and why,
not just showing the case where they work.

### Projects

**[robotArm-PPO-RL](https://github.com/alvegonza/robotArm-PPO-RL)**
A 4-joint arm I modelled in MuJoCo that learns to reach a cube and push it to a
target with PPO. Next step is feeding it positions from a camera instead of the
simulator's ground truth, and seeing how much that hurts.

**[where-detectors-fail](https://github.com/alvegonza/where-detectors-fail)**
I hand-labelled 154 people in a scene from *1917* and ran around 50 detector
configurations against it. Tuning YOLO took recall from 30% to 65%. Switching to
RT-DETR did more than all of that tuning combined.

**[soma-reccmi-RAG](https://github.com/alvegonza/soma-reccmi-RAG)**
My bachelor's thesis. A RAG assistant over 435 Spanish internal-medicine case
reports, evaluated on 1,731 questions. The biomedical model came out worst,
which I didn't expect.
